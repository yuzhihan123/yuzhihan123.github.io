Bristol Pound

PROJECT

A Piece of Bristol uses the Bristol Pound as its narrative thread, moving through three urban spaces—the market, a community garden, and the harbour—to portray Bristol's connection, community, and culture. It explores how point-of-view design, guided sound, and symbolic interaction can create a sense of immersion within a non-interactive 360° film.
